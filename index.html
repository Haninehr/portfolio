About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate from the University of Algiers 1, specializing in Information Systems Engineering (ISII).

My work sits at the intersection of software engineering and artificial intelligence. I enjoy designing complete products, building their frontend and backend, and integrating AI where it genuinely improves the experience.

My most significant project is Beez AI, a startup-oriented smart beekeeping ecosystem developed with my co-founder. It combines hive-management tools with an AI assistant built around retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

I'm especially interested in machine learning, intelligent systems, applied research, and building technology with measurable practical value.
