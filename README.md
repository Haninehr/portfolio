## About Me

I'm a Computer Science graduate from the **University of Algiers 1**, specializing in **Information Systems Engineering (ISII)**. My work sits at the intersection of software engineering and artificial intelligence — I enjoy designing complete products, building their frontend and backend, and integrating AI where it genuinely improves the experience.

My most significant project is **Beez AI**, a startup-oriented smart beekeeping ecosystem developed with my co-founder. It combines hive-management tools with an AI assistant built around retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). I'm especially interested in machine learning, intelligent systems, applied research, and building technology with measurable practical value.

- 🔭 **Currently building**: **Beez AI** — a smart beekeeping ecosystem with a RAG-powered assistant
- 🌱 **Exploring**: RAG evaluation, AI agents, computer vision, and blockchain/fintech
- 💬 **Ask me about**: Flutter, Spring Boot, AI integration, or full-stack development
- 📫 **Reach me**: [Rouibah.Hanine@gmail.com](mailto:Rouibah.Hanine@gmail.com)
